About

About the Fair

Language is one of humanity's most remarkable abilities. Through language, we understand one another, record history, tell stories, and drive civilization forward. Linguistics research blends the rigorous analytical methods of natural science with the humanistic empathy of the social sciences — making it a uniquely interdisciplinary field. We invite all middle school students in Taiwan to explore language through their own creative research questions and share their findings with peers.

🔬

Scientific Inquiry

Investigate language phenomena around you, formulate your own research questions, and experience the joy of linguistic research firsthand.

📝

Paper & Poster

Present your work as a short research paper and a poster at the conference. Any creative, language-related topic is welcome!

🎓

Eligibility

Open to all middle/high school students in Taiwan (including international schools and home-schooled students). Teams of up to 3 students are allowed.

🌏

IOL Qualification

Top 8 finalists will receive qualification for the 2027 Taiwan IOL Selection Round 2, advancing toward the international linguistics olympiad.

Paper Format

Paper Format Guidelines

Please ensure your submission meets all formatting requirements before uploading.

Basic Requirements

  • Language: Chinese or English
  • Paper size: A4
  • Chinese submissions: max 6 pages (incl. appendices, excl. cover)
  • English submissions: max 9 pages (incl. appendices, excl. cover)

Typography & Formatting

  • Chinese font: 標楷體 (Biao Kai)
  • English font: Times New Roman
  • Headings: 14 pt
  • Body text: 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing
  • Margins: 2.54 cm on all sides

Cover Page (separate page)

  • Paper title
  • Author name(s)
  • School name and grade
  • Faculty advisor

Paper Structure (in order)

  • Introduction
  • Literature Review
  • Method
  • Results and Analyses
  • Conclusion
  • References (APA format)
  • Appendices
📁 Filename format: Title_AuthorNames.pdf
Example: Investigating_the_Language_of_Martians_Chen_Lin_Huang.pdf
Judging Criteria

Judging Criteria

Papers are evaluated across six dimensions

💡

Research Topic

Relevant to linguistics and demonstrates originality

📖

Introduction

Clearly articulates the research background, motivation, objectives, and research questions

📚

Literature Review

Objective and clear, with appropriate citations of relevant literature

🧪

Methodology

Clearly describes data collection and analysis procedures

📊

Results & Analysis

Reasonable, complete, and logically consistent; figures and tables are accurate and results are interpreted appropriately

✅

Conclusion & References

Conclusion addresses the research questions and offers insights; references follow APA format and are complete
